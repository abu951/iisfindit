School Lost & Found

Lost your water bottle? Find it in a minute.

Every week, bottles get left in the canteen, on the playground and on the bus. FindIt puts every found item and every lost report in one place, so bottles get back to the people who need them.

    The problem

    Water bottles disappear every day, and nobody knows where they went.

    In the UAE heat students need to drink all day. A lost bottle means thirsty lessons, a replacement bought, and one more bottle that ends up in the bin.

    They all look alike

    Dozens of blue and silver bottles sit on the same shelf. Without a name or a sticker, nobody can prove which one is theirs.

    There is no single place to look

    Items end up in the canteen, the playground, the bus bay and the office. Students give up before they have checked them all.

    Lost things become waste

    Bottles left for weeks are thrown away, and families buy new ones. Reuse is better for money and for the planet.

    How it works

    Three steps from lost to returned

    1. Report it

      Lost something? Tell us what it looks like and where you last had it. Found something? Add it with a photo and hand it to the Front Office.

    2. Match it

      FindIt compares new reports with items already on the board and suggests the closest matches by type, colour and place.

    3. Claim it

      Press "This is mine", describe one detail only the owner knows, and get a claim ticket. Show it at the Front Office to collect your item.

    The board

    Browse found items and lost reports

    Report

    Tell us what you lost or found

    It takes about a minute. Use your first name and class only. Never write phone numbers or home addresses.

    What are you reporting?

    Do not type the full name or the ID number. Staff will check the card when it is handed in.

    Keep one secret detail to yourself, like a name inside the lid. The owner will need it to claim the item.

    Photo (optional, but it helps a lot)

    Photograph the item on its own. Keep faces and name tags out of the picture.

    Found something?

    Add it here, then hand it to the Front Office and show your tag ID. Please do not keep found items in your bag or locker.

    Lost something?

    • Check the board first. It may already be here.
    • Check again each morning. New items arrive daily.
    • Ask your class teacher if it has been a week.

    Stop it before it starts

    Four ways to keep your bottle yours

    Write your name inside the lid

    A marker works on most lids. Names on the outside fade, so use the inside too.

    Add a sticker or strap

    One bright sticker makes your bottle different from the forty on the shelf.

    Take a quick photo at home

    A clear photo helps you describe it and helps staff recognise it.

    Do a "three-point check"

    Before you leave a room, check bottle, bag, jumper. It takes five seconds.

    Why we built FindIt

    Problem
    Students often lose water bottles, lunch boxes and clothing, and there is no easy way to find them.
    Solution
    One shared board where found items and lost reports meet, with matching suggestions and a safe claim ticket.
    Impact
    Fewer replacement purchases, less waste, and more students with water when they need it. This supports the UN goal of responsible consumption (SDG 12).
    Next steps
    Run a trial with one grade, count returns each week, and share results with the school.
